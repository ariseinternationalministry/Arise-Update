01A warm welcome

Thank you for visiting Arise International Ministry (“Arise”, “we”, “us”). We are a Christian ministry based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and an online fellowship on Telegram and Zoom. We share the gospel, teach God’s Word, train believers and serve people in need across Ethiopia.

Your trust matters to us. We keep things simple because we collect very little.

02The short version

  • We don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies on this website.
  • We only receive what you choose to share — for example through the contact form, a call, WhatsApp or email.
  • We use it to reply to you, to pray with you and to serve you. Nothing else.
  • We never sell your information, and we never ask for passwords, card numbers or PINs.

03What you may share with us

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone (optional) and your message.
  • Calls, WhatsApp, Telegram and email: your name, number or username, and whatever you tell us — for example when you sign up for a hike, training or conference.
  • Prayer requests and testimonies: share only what you’re comfortable with. Please keep other people’s private details out unless they’re happy for you to share them.
  • Giving confirmations: if you send us a receipt after a gift, we see what is on it, such as your name, the amount and the reference.

This site doesn’t set cookies. Like any website, our web host may keep basic technical logs (such as browser type and time of visit) to keep the site safe and running. We don’t use them to identify visitors.

04How we use it

  • To answer your questions and to pray with you.
  • To sign you up for events, trainings and programs, and tell you if anything changes.
  • To thank you for a gift and keep good ministry records.
  • To invite you to serve or partner, when you’ve asked about it.
  • To keep the website safe and working well.

We only send ministry updates if you ask for them, and you can stop them any time.

05Who may see it

Only the people who need to, so we can help you:

  • Our ministry team, to reply to you or run an event.
  • Everyday tools that carry our messages and site, such as email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Zoom, our web host and Google Fonts (your browser asks Google for the fonts when the page loads).
  • Your bank, Telebirr or PayPal, when you give. They handle your gift under their own terms.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect someone from harm.

Links to Facebook, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp and Google Maps take you to their own services, which have their own privacy policies.

06When you give

Our account details are on the Give page, including local bank and Telebirr details and, for friends abroad, PayPal and international bank transfer (SWIFT). Your gift goes straight through your bank, Telebirr or PayPal — never through this website — so we don’t collect your card or account details. We see only what the payment shows the receiver, such as your name and the amount, and we keep gift records for thankfulness, accounting and legal reasons.

07Photos, video and stories

We love sharing stories so supporters can see the gospel at work. We do it with care and respect, especially for children, prisoners and anyone vulnerable. If you’re in a photo and would rather not be, just tell us and we’ll remove it quickly.

08Keeping it safe, and for how long

We keep information only as long as we need it, then delete it or make it anonymous. We protect it by limiting who can see it. Because no online method is perfectly secure, please don’t send ID numbers, passwords or card details by form or WhatsApp.

09Your choices

You’re always in control. Under Ethiopian law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything that’s wrong;
  • delete your information or stop using it;
  • stop sending you messages.

Just write to Ariseinternationalministry@gmail.com. We may ask a quick question to confirm it’s you, then we’ll reply as soon as we can.

10Young friends

This site is for everyone. If you’re under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sharing personal details or signing up for an event. If a child has shared information with us by mistake, tell us and we’ll delete it.

11If things change

If we ever update this page (for example, when we add a new tool), we’ll change the “Last updated” date above. We always aim to keep it short and kind.

01Welcome

Thank you for visiting the Arise International Ministry website (the “Site”). By using it, you agree to these simple terms and our Privacy Policy. If anything here doesn’t sit right with you, just reach out — we’re happy to talk.

02What this Site is for

The Site shares the work of Arise — our teaching, training, events, mission stories and ways to give or get involved. It’s here to inform and to encourage you in your walk with God.

03Using the Site kindly

We ask that everyone use the Site with respect. Please:

  • be kind and honest in anything you send us;
  • don’t send spam, harmful files or hateful messages;
  • don’t pretend to be Arise or use our name to collect money;
  • don’t try to break, overload or get unauthorised access to the Site.

04Giving

  • Every gift is voluntary and deeply appreciated. Gifts support evangelism, discipleship, prison ministry, training and compassion. Where you give for a specific purpose and we’re able to honour it, we will; otherwise our leadership directs it where the need is greatest.
  • You can give by bank transfer, Telebirr, and — from abroad — by PayPal or international bank transfer (SWIFT).
  • Please double-check the name, number or email before you send, and use only the details on our official Give page. If anyone asks you to give in our name using different details, please check with us first.
  • Your bank, Telebirr or PayPal processes your payment under its own terms, including any fees or timing. We can’t control those.
  • If you gave by mistake, tell us as soon as you can and we’ll do our best to help.
  • Giving is a gift of love — it doesn’t buy any service, status or benefit.

05Events, hikes and trainings

  • Dates, times, places, prices and details may change. We’ll let registered friends know as soon as we can.
  • Places can be limited, and some events have early-registration offers.
  • Outdoor activities like hiking are physical and involve natural risks. You join voluntarily and look after your own health and belongings. Please tell the organisers about any medical needs, dress and carry water well, and follow the team leaders. Young people should come with a parent or guardian’s permission.
  • We may take photos and video at events for ministry use. If you’d rather not appear, just tell the organisers.
  • If we cancel an event for safety, weather or other reasons, we’ll refund any fees or move them to a new date.

06Our content

The words, photographs, designs and logos on this Site belong to Arise International Ministry or are used with permission. You’re welcome to share links and quote short extracts with credit. Please don’t sell, change or reuse our content commercially without asking first.

Bank, Telebirr and PayPal names shown on the Give page belong to their owners and are used only to help you find the right way to give.

07What you send us

When you send us a message, prayer request or testimony, you’re confirming it’s yours to share. We’ll use it to respond to you and we won’t publish your story or name without your permission.

08Teaching and spiritual content

Our teaching is offered for spiritual growth and encouragement. It isn’t medical, legal, financial or other professional advice, so please seek a qualified professional for those needs.

09Links to other sites

We link to services such as Facebook, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp, PayPal and Google Maps for your convenience. They have their own terms and we can’t control them.

10Good to know

We work hard to keep the Site accurate and available, but we can’t promise it will always be error-free or uninterrupted. We’re not responsible for losses that come from using third-party services linked from the Site. Nothing here limits any right you have by law.

11If we update these terms

We may refresh these terms from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version, and we’ll always aim to keep them short and kind.

12Ethiopian law

These terms follow the laws of Ethiopia. If a concern ever comes up, we’d love to talk it through with you first and resolve it peacefully. If that isn’t possible, the competent courts of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia will handle it.

13Say hello

Questions about these terms? Email Ariseinternationalministry@gmail.com or call +251993002237. We’re based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. See also our Privacy Policy.
